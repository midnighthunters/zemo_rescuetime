Food Truck World Tour

Users walk to unlock ingredients, dishes, and cities.

Example:

1,000 steps = unlock tomato
3,000 steps = cook pizza
7,000 steps = open food truck
15,000 steps = unlock New York / Tokyo / Paris

Why exciting: food, travel, collecting, progression.

Users can build:

Pizza truck
Taco truck
Sushi truck
Burger truck
Dessert truck

This can become very addictive because people love unlocking food items.
